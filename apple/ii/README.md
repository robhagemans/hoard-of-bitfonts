Apple II fonts
==============

These are the fonts used by the various Apple II versions:
- Apple ][
- Apple //e
- Apple //e (Enhanced) and Apple //c
- Apple IIGS (Apple II compatibility mode)

International versions:
- Apple ][ JPlus (Japanese)
- Apple //e UK, French, German
- Apple //e (Enhanced) Hebrew

Apple II clones:
- Agat-7 and -9 (Soviet)
- Pravetz-82, -8A/-8C, -8M (Bulgarian)
