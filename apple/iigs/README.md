Apple IIGS ProDOS 16 and GS/OS fonts
====================================

The Apple IIGS was the last model in the Apple II range. It was a 16-bit machine and therefore substantially different from its 8-bit predecessors, but it 
maintained compatibility through a kind of system-on-a-chip emulation. 

Its operating system was first ProDOS16 (System 1.0 -- 3.2) and later GS/OS (System 4.0 -- 6.0.1).
This directory contains the fonts shipped with these systems. The fonts were provided in separate files, except Shaston 8 which was built-in in ROM.

Up to and including System 5.x, the following fonts were included:

- Shaston 8, 16
- Courier 10, 12
- Geneva 10, 12
- Helvetica 10, 12
- Times 10, 12
- Venice 14

The other sizes included here shipped from System 6.x onwards.


Appke II compatibility font
===========================

Mega II, the system-on-a-chip that maintained compatibility with the 8-bit Apple II line, also included a font very similar to the ROM fonts of the 8-bit Apple II models.
It is included in the Apple II folder.
