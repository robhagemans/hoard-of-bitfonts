Standard Microsystems Corporation CRT 8002 Video Display Attributes Controller / Video Generator
================================================================================================

The CRT-8002 is used in Steve Ciarcia's "ZAP" computer from the book
[Build Your Own Z80 Computer](https://books.google.com/books?id=mVQnFgWzX0AC&printsec=frontcover#v=onepage&q&f=false).
It is also used in the SuperBrain Video Computer System.

The glyphs here have been copied from or checked against [the datasheet](https://rocelec.widen.net/view/pdf/r6gtaq06b2/SMSCS01026-1.pdf).
Glyph metrics are based on the information provided there. Note however that the glyphs have been shifted to their typographical baseline.
